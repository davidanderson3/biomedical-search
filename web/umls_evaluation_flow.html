Heuristic rule example

Prefer complete terms

Observed

heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

The standalone concept Reduced ranked 6.

Rule

Penalize a candidate whose label is only a modifier—such as reduced, elevated, low, high, mild, or severe—when the query contains other specific terms.

Result

Reduced moved to rank 35. Complete heart-failure and ejection-fraction concepts remained highly ranked.
